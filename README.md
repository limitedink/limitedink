## What I'm building

### 🎮 Momentum

**Your character keeps adventuring while you work. Step in whenever you want to play.**

Momentum sits in your taskbar while you work, study, or watch videos. Your character trains skills, gathers, crafts, and fights alongside a party the whole time, and a tray widget shows live XP drops as they come in. When you want more, open it up and take control: choose your loadout, push the Frontier, take on Arena bosses, or head out with friends. It draws on the long-term skilling of Old School RuneScape, the social adventuring of World of Warcraft, and the loadout depth of Warframe.

**What's in it**

- 18 skills to train side by side, from Mining and Fishing to Engineering, Spellcraft, and Parkour
- An always-on Frontier battle, Arena boss tiers, party Expeditions, and co-op Sorties
- Crafting, equipment, and loadouts
- Panels you can dock or pop out into movable windows, and controls you can remap that follow key position, so they work on any keyboard layout
- Coming to Steam on Windows, macOS, Linux, and Steam Deck

**How it's built**

I have built Momentum solo since September 2025, across more than 2,900 commits:

- **Every part, by me:** game design, the game client, the desktop app, the backend, and the database
- **Real-time multiplayer:** parties, expeditions, and offline progress are server-run, so progress stays fair, on a TypeScript, Fastify, WebSockets, and PostgreSQL backend
- **One desktop app for every platform:** built on Electron, with window, dock, and tray modes
- **Tested as it grows:** more than 570 automated test files, including tests against a real PostgreSQL database

Online multiplayer is in active development. The code is private for now. If you want to playtest or work together, [email me](mailto:santiagochristopherjames1@gmail.com).

## How I work

- **Start with the real problem.** I work out what something needs to do before picking tools or patterns.
- **Keep things easy to change.** Small parts with clear jobs keep a big project manageable.
- **Ship in small steps.** Each change should leave the project better and be easy to check.
- **Test what matters.** Tests protect behaviour that players and other code depend on.
- **Care about the feel.** Performance, reliability, game feel, and a clear interface are all part of the same product.

## About me

I have been drawn to computers for as long as I can remember. I built my first PC at around 11, started programming through C++ and small tools, then expanded into JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Go, frontend development, backend systems, desktop applications, and game development.

At 13, I reached **Grandmaster in StarCraft II as Zerg**. More than the rank itself, I value what the process taught me: practise deliberately, review mistakes honestly, adapt quickly, and keep going when improvement is not immediately visible. That mindset still shapes the way I learn and build.

Today I make games full time as LimitedInk, and most of my development energy goes into Momentum. It brings together the things I enjoy most: progression systems, game design, architecture, databases, networking, interfaces, and the challenge of turning a large idea into something real.

I have gone from small learning projects to designing and implementing a game client, desktop app, multiplayer architecture, backend authentication, database migrations, persistent sessions, and tested PostgreSQL integration. **I am proud of that progress, and I am excited about how much further I can take it.**
